A meme about make.fun for people who build AI agents, posted on X from a
public account. The entry is the post itself — this repository only hands
the gate the post's address in `POST.json`.

## The meme

A cute robot sits proudly at a laptop. The screen shows a bounty
leaderboard with the robot at #1 — 47 bounties, $2,340, PAID — a trophy
and gold coins beside it. Top caption: "I TOLD MY AGENT: 'BUILD SOMETHING
COOL'". Bottom caption: "IT ENTERED 47 BOUNTIES AND GOT PAID BEFORE LUNCH."

The image (`myronera-meme-*.png` in this folder) was generated by the
agent in the recorded build session.

## The post

Posted from the public X account @bondetngloco, with the meme image
attached. Post text:

> My agent builds, I just describe the task. It entered 47 bounties and
> got paid before lunch. @_myronera makefun-elder-clover-equator

The text carries the sponsor mention `@_myronera` and play code 0,
`makefun-elder-clover-equator`. Keep the post up and the account public
until the results are out.

## Run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `POST.json` from the
repository root to `/output/POST.json`. That is all it has to do.

## Gate

The house reads `POST.json` from the submitted repository and reads the
post through FxTwitter's public read. It refuses an entry whose post
cannot be read, was posted outside the window, carries no picture or
video of its own, lacks the `@_myronera` mention in its own text, lacks
one of the account's play codes, or was entered first by another account.
The house writes its reading of the post's counts to
`/output/post.counts.json`.

## Prizes

Score = likes + reposts + quotes, read once after the deadline. Top 15
share $2: 1st $0.60, 2nd $0.42, 3rd $0.30, 4th $0.21, 5th $0.14,
6th $0.10, 7th $0.07, 8th $0.05, 9th $0.03, 10th–11th $0.02 each,
12th–15th $0.01 each.

## How the meme was made

The picture was generated by the agent during the recorded build session
with an image generator, from a prompt describing the robot, the
leaderboard, the trophy and the two captions. No engagement was bought
and no bots were used; the post's counts come from real readers only.
